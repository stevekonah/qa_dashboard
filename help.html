Overview

The QA Dashboard monitors quality across all active Pathfinder projects using a standard checklist submitted through KoboToolbox. Data refreshes daily at 06:00 UTC.

Key Metrics

MetricDefinition
Overall QA ScoreAverage of all scored answers across submissions
Submission ScorePercentage of compliant answers in one checklist
On TrackLatest score ≥ 80%
Needs AttentionLatest score 60–79%
At RiskLatest score < 60%
ConcernNegative answer to a question flagged as a concern
Risk Score(Concerns × 2) + ((1 − Score) × 10)
Risk CategoryCritical ≥ 10 · High ≥ 6 · Medium ≥ 3 · Low < 3
Programmatic ScoreAverage of Programmatic-pillar answers
MEL ScoreAverage of MEL-pillar answers
Startup / Implementation / Closeout ScoreAverage of questions in that project stage

Scoring Methodology

  • Each question is scored 1 (compliant) or 0 (non-compliant).
  • All questions carry equal weight.
  • Reverse-scored questions are already reflected in the Score column of the Scoring Checklist.
  • Questions with no Score value are excluded.

FAQ

How often does data refresh? Daily at 06:00 UTC.

Why is a high-scoring project still At Risk? The Risk Score weights concerns heavily.

How do I drill into a project? Click any row in "All Projects" or use the sidebar link.
